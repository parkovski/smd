About

Parker Snell started programming and creating websites at the age of 12. He has extensive experience in solving technological problems. He has worked at the University of Arizona HiRISE project, Facebook (now Meta), and Microsoft. Throughout his career, he has worked on numerous hobby projects, many of which are available on his personal website and his GitHub.

As a programmer, Parker specializes in server-side work, though he is also capable of developing frontend-focused websites. Parker is an expert in JavaScript, C++, and C#, and is familiar with many different frameworks spanning all kinds of different technological needs, and will work with you to find the best fit for the job.
